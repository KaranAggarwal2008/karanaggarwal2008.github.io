Research, analysis and writing
2026 / collaborative empirical research

Strategic Under-Pricing in Online Auctions

Co-authored empirical analysis of 628 historical auction observations plus 200 newly collected eBay listings (187 usable for reputation analysis). Used OLS and HC3 robust inference to test starting-price effects and moderators, finding no support for the simplistic low-starting-price premium. SISTER/YEL research team with Tanishk Garg, Aidana Seitova and Advay Panyala.

2026 / geopolitical finance

The Uri Attack and Indian Stock Market

Conducted YRI-mentored analysis of Indian market responses around September 2016 using event windows, alternative dates and difference-in-differences methods. Accepted for presentation consideration at the World Finance & Banking Symposium 2026.

2026 / policy history

From State Primacy to Startup Facilitation

Co-authored an NSRI Finelytics documentary study of Indian enterprise policy, 1956–2025, with Basma Ehab Basuoiny and Namratha Chowdary Mogalapu. Developed legal-policy timelines and methodological revisions in response to feedback.

2026 / model-based research

SKILLFLOW

Developed and iteratively revised a model of endogenous milestones and contract incentives in vocational financing, including reproducible sensitivity analysis. Manuscript under development and submission, not described as published.
