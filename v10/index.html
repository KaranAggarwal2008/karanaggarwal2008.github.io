A little more human

When I finished school, I thought I would finally have some time to relax.

That did not really happen.

I moved between classes, research drafts, conferences and collaborative projects. But the best parts were rarely the lines that eventually made it onto an application: a conversation after a session, someone taking a chance on an idea, or the moment a problem stopped looking simple.

I still enjoy writing, music, travel, tinkering with websites and finding new people to learn from. The point of this site is to leave room for those things, too.
