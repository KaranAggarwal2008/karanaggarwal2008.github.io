03 / Next

The question isn't just what comes next.

It's what I want to keep learning, who I want to work with, and how I can make the work matter outside a document or competition room. I don't have a final, perfectly linear answer. That's part of what makes the future interesting.

From school to a wider classroom

Still a student. Still building.

After finishing school in 2026, I began studying through IIM Bangalore's BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship programme, while continuing university coursework, research projects and collaboration with people across countries.

One thing I appreciate about an unconventional path is the chance to test an interest rather than only declare it. A finance course can make a question about public policy more precise. A research project can reveal an organisational problem. A student venture can teach you more about institutions than a theoretical essay.

For now, I'm especially interested in economics, international relations, entrepreneurship and how emerging technologies change what governments, organisations and individuals can do. I don't think they need to be mutually exclusive.

What I'm currently learning

An education built across contexts.

2026 onward / IIM Bangalore

Digital Business and Entrepreneurship

I'm enrolled in IIM Bangalore's BBA DBE programme. The coursework spans entrepreneurship, marketing, business communication, financial statements, statistics and website development. The site you're reading is also a practical exercise in applying the latter: information architecture, HTML, CSS, storytelling and attention to how someone actually navigates a page.

2026 / university summer courses

Studying across disciplines

Harvard Summer School courses in Money & Markets and Public Finance, Yale's Introduction to Psychology, Johns Hopkins' Elements of Macroeconomics and Cornell's Global Business Strategy exposed me to different methods, expectations and arguments. Each influenced the questions I later brought to my own projects.

2027 / professional study

Company secretary entrance pathway

I completed the CSEET Student Induction Programme and registered for the February 2027 entrance examination through ICSI. I'm treating it as ongoing learning in the legal and governance side of business, not a completed professional qualification.
