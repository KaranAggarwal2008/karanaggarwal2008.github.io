AN ECONOMIC FRAMEWORK

Technology can raise productivity without equalising opportunity.

The question behind the essay

When AI tools increase productivity, who benefits first? Adoption depends on skills, access, complementarity with existing work and the power to capture the gains. This work began as an economics essay before becoming a public theoretical preprint.

Why adjustment can be uneven

Firms and workers differ in their access to tools, ability to reorganise tasks and exposure to displacement. An aggregate productivity gain can therefore coexist with adjustment costs, unequal bargaining positions and changes in the distribution of income or opportunities.

What the framework does

The paper sets out possible mechanisms linking technical adoption to uneven economic adjustment. It does not claim that one new dataset has identified a universal effect for every sector or country. Rather, it offers an argument that can guide more targeted empirical questions.

Public preprint

I posted the working version to SSRN so readers could access the framework and evaluate its assumptions.
