CO-AUTHORED CHAPTER

Finance education, AI and sustainable choices.

The question

Green investment decisions depend on more than willingness to do good. Young people need to understand financial products, assess claims about sustainability and make sense of rapidly expanding streams of advice, some of it shaped by AI tools.

A connected framework

The chapter brings together financial education, technological access and sustainable investment choices among Indian youth. It explores how literacy and the quality of information may affect people's ability to interpret investment opportunities and the language of sustainability.

Co-authorship

I wrote this chapter with Utsav Singh. It is a co-authored, published book chapter, not my sole-authored journal article and not the same as the SSRN preprints elsewhere in the research archive.
