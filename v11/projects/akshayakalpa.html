Scaling Akshayakalpa High-Protein Milk

An operational growth case balancing distribution, demand and wastage.

What we were asked to solve

Our IIM Bangalore case centred on Akshayakalpa Organic's high-protein milk. We were asked to design a plan to grow from approximately 3,100 litres per day to 10,000 litres per day by March 2027, while limiting product wastage and the risk of platform delisting. It wasn't enough to assume customers would suddenly appear because the target was ambitious.

How we framed a response

We explored the relationship between customer acquisition, quick-commerce discovery, subscription retention, inventory planning and channel-specific incentives. One recommendation was to make subscription offers visible where customers were already deciding whether to order again, rather than treating a subscription as a separate product no one could find.

Because the case had cost constraints, our analysis also considered targeted promotions and the importance of avoiding broad spending that would increase demand without improving fulfilment or retention.
