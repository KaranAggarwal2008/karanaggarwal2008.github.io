DPS CHANDIGARH · 2025–26

Deepshikha — the student company

A school enterprise with shareholders, stalls, team decisions and financial literacy.

The context

We ran a school company with real shareholders, products, events and money to account for.

My role

I served as Managing Director from April 2025 to March 2026, coordinating a roughly 50-member team and five stalls.

What we did

I led sales and operations planning, team coordination and shareholder communication. I also co-convened Financial Literacy Week, with 45 volunteers across 49 rooms and activities involving more than 1,000 students.

The result

Our company reported 540% profit. Financial Literacy Week reached more than 1,000 students, and the programme combined sales experience with practical financial education.

Team & collaborators

Student company team of approximately 50; supported by teachers and student contributors

In my words

The Instagram photographs show what the company looked like day to day: planning meetings, stalls, sales and events. I learned as much from organising the people as from the numbers.
