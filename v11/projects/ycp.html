IIM RANCHI · 2025

Young Changemakers Programme

A rejection, a return, and Project Sweet Chilli in rural Jharkhand.

The context

I applied to YCP 3.0, was not selected for the programme, and tried again. YCP 4.0 took me to Jharkhand for village immersion.

My role

I was a Young Changemaker on Team Drishti; previously I had been recognised as an Outreach Champion for YCP 3.0.

What we did

In Lohajimi, we investigated elephant-related crop damage and proposed Project Sweet Chilli. I took part in community observation, discussions and the presentation.

The result

Team Drishti finished among the top three of 17 teams at YCP 4.0.

Team & collaborators

Gargi Patankar, Avya Khurana, Prakash Kumar, Vibhuti Patil, Karmishtha Dabral; mentor Dev Tiwari

In my words

YCP made the difference between designing a solution on slides and actually seeing how people experience a problem very clear to me.
