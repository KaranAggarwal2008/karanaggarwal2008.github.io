LAUNCHX ENTREPRENEURSHIP · 2026

OnPace — LaunchX Entrepreneurship

A study platform concept tested with paying customers.

The context

We wanted to bring studying, scheduling and organisation into a product that students would actually use.

My role

I helped build and pitch OnPace with Allen Yu, Hakan Atahan Aydın, Yashi Sodha and Zhongyun Zhang.

What we did

We developed the product concept, tested whether users would pay and coordinated work across time zones.

The result

Our team generated about US$260 in revenue over six weeks, finished second on the LaunchX Entrepreneurship leaderboard and received Best Pitch.

Team & collaborators

Allen Yu, Hakan Atahan Aydın, Yashi Sodha and Zhongyun Zhang

The venture and the people

OnPace was our attempt to reduce the friction around planning work and staying organised. It was built through LaunchX Online Entrepreneurship in 2026, where a product wasn't only something to describe: we had to test a proposition, adjust based on responses and try to generate real customer interest.

From planning to a sale

We worked across time zones, refined the offer and learned that agreement within a team was not the same as evidence of customer demand. The venture reported approximately $260 in sales during six weeks. That isn't a huge corporate revenue number, and it shouldn't be presented as one. For us, it was a tangible signal that somebody outside the team saw value in what we were offering.

The programme culminated in a second-place leaderboard position and a Best Pitch recognition. I took away a sharper appreciation of why clear communication, direct feedback and small execution details matter.
