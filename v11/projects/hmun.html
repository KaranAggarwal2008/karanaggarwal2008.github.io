HMUN India — BuildScore

At HMUN India in Bengaluru, our team presented BuildScore as an Impact Initiative proposal and reached the finalist stage.

The context

The Impact Initiative challenged us to turn a social problem into a concrete, explainable proposal.

My role

I worked on the concept and pitch with the team while also participating as a delegate at HMUN India.

What we did

We developed BuildScore, prepared a social-impact pitch and presented it during the conference in Bengaluru.

The result

BuildScore reached the final seven Impact Initiative pitches.

Team & collaborators

I worked with a project team on BuildScore at HMUN India in Bengaluru.

In my words

I joined HMUN India in Bengaluru for its committee sessions and Impact Initiative. BuildScore was the project we brought to the Impact Initiative; the HPAIR Dynamic Policy Simulation and Best Speaker Award were from an entirely different conference in Hanoi.
