HANOI · AUGUST 2026

HPAIR Asia — Policy Simulation

Semiconductor negotiations, ASEAN autonomy and a Best Speaker award.

The context

In Hanoi, the Dynamic Policy Simulation challenged us to negotiate semiconductor and industrial-policy decisions across competing interests.

My role

I represented the China Coalition in the simulation and participated in negotiations and policy discussions.

What we did

I argued stakeholder positions, responded to competing priorities and negotiated during the dynamic simulation.

The result

I received the Best Speaker Award for the Dynamic Policy Simulation at HPAIR Asia Conference 2026.

Team & collaborators

Conference delegates and policy simulation participants

In my words

HPAIR brought me into conversations with participants from different countries. Negotiating in a policy simulation required a different kind of preparation from a conventional speech.
