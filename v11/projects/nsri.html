From a timeline to an argument

Our Finelytics paper, From State Primacy to Startup Facilitation: India's Policy Evolution (1956–2025), grew from the National Student Research Initiative research hackathon. Instead of treating India's startup ecosystem as a single recent policy success story, we examined the longer movement from state-led industrialisation and controls through liberalisation and modern entrepreneurship-support institutions.

The historical arc mattered because a policy introduced in 2025 cannot, by itself, explain changes in business registrations or startup outcomes several years earlier. We needed to separate chronology, mechanism and causality rather than treating dates as interchangeable.

Methods and changes after feedback

The manuscript developed through reading primary policy documents, constructing a timeline and connecting legal changes to shifts in institutional priorities. Reviewer feedback challenged us to define a reproducible document-coding rubric, broaden Indian legal and policy sources and avoid treating the 2025 tax framework as a cause of 2016–2024 outcomes.

It was useful criticism. A persuasive historical narrative still needs a transparent method: why a document was included, how its claims were classified, and what the evidence can actually support.
