SBI Scholar Quiz — The Team Behind the Win

From Chandigarh city winners to the national semifinals.

The competition

Financial quizzes were a way of bringing together current affairs, economics and the kind of recall I'd been practising for years. At the SBI Scholar Quiz, my official competition partner was Mehar Uberoi. We won the Chandigarh city stage and progressed into the national top sixteen, where we finished fourth in our semifinal.
