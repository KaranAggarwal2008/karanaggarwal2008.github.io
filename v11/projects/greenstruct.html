STARTUP PITCH · 2024–25

GreenStruct — IIT Bombay Eureka Junior

A student venture pitch that changed as the team, the idea and the feedback evolved.

How it began

GreenStruct was part of my early attempts to turn a business idea into something a competition jury could evaluate. Rather than treating the idea as finished once we'd named it, we worked through pitch materials, explanations and presentation revisions during the IIT Bombay Eureka Junior process.

The final working team

Our merged final team included me, Raghav Gadhyan and Manan Agarwal. That distinction matters because I worked with other people named Raghav in different competitions around the same period.

Raghav Gadhyan

Final GreenStruct teammate at IIT Bombay Eureka Junior, also involved in later competition exploration including Founder's Grid.

Manan Agarwal

Member of our final merged GreenStruct team during Eureka Junior.

Outcome and learning

GreenStruct reached the competition's top-300 finalist group from a reported field of 81,250 entries. What I remember is learning how quickly an outsider can find the gap between the idea we think we've explained and the one they've actually understood. Working on the pitch taught me to cut unnecessary language and put the problem before the product.
