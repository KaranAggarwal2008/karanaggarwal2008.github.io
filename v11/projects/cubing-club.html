Cubing Club — Reviving a School Community

A small project about keeping a shared interest alive.

Giving the right credit

The school Cubing Club was founded by Suhani Sharma. I later worked with Aastha Gupta to revive it. Aastha and Pulkit Malik continued the activities afterwards. The club's history matters because an organisation rarely belongs to a single person who happens to describe it on a résumé.

What a club needs to survive

A school club depends on people knowing when to show up, having opportunities to practise and feeling comfortable even if they're not the fastest in the room. Reviving one isn't simply relaunching a title. It requires someone to organise a session, maintain momentum and hand responsibilities to the next group.
