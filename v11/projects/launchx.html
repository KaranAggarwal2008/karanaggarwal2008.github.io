LaunchX Innovation — MKE Tech

An employee-efficiency challenge and a Best Pitch result.

The context

At LaunchX Innovation, our team worked on an employee-efficiency challenge set by MKE Tech.

My role

I worked with Nathaniel Macalalad, Davin Phan and Shubh Singhal on framing the problem and preparing the pitch.

What we did

We examined the employee-efficiency problem, developed an approach and presented it to the programme.

The result

Our team received Best Pitch and finished first on the LaunchX Innovation programme leaderboard.

Team & collaborators

Nathaniel Macalalad, Davin Phan and Shubh Singhal

In my words

This was the MKE Tech innovation challenge, separate from the OnPace entrepreneurship programme.
