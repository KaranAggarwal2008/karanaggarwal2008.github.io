LEGAL ENTREPRENEURSHIP · NOVEMBER 2024

Samviddhi 6.0 — Jet Airways

A legal-business case with Tejas Singh.

The problem we explored

For the legal-entrepreneurship component of Samviddhi 6.0, Tejas Singh and I developed a submission based on the Jet Airways case. It required looking beyond the simple story of an airline's difficulties and considering how business decisions, legal institutions and stakeholders interact.

What we submitted

We prepared and submitted a presentation and supporting PDF, with materials sent in November 2024. The work asked us to make a structured argument under competition constraints. A case presentation can be persuasive without being simplistic; that balance was part of what we practised.

The collaboration
Tejas Singh

My partner on the Jet Airways Samviddhi 6.0 submission. We also worked together on Deepshikha, Shemrock House of Trivia, Banyan Tree and other activities.

Tejas and I had already spent time preparing for quizzes and student projects together. The advantage was knowing how the other person worked; the risk was assuming we had communicated a task when we hadn't. This competition sharpened that habit.
