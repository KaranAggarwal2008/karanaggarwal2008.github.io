Schoolhouse.world

Tutoring, dialogue, writing and curriculum work across countries.

The context

Schoolhouse.world brings learners and volunteers together for tutoring and dialogue across borders.

My role

I am a Peer Tutor, Blogs Editor and Dialogues Club President.

What we did

I have supported more than 200 learners across 40 countries and facilitated more than 3,000 minutes of dialogue sessions; I also contributed to curriculum discussions.

The result

My work combines mathematics tutoring, dialogue facilitation and writing/editing in an international volunteer community.

Team & collaborators

Peer tutors, Schoolhouse Dialogue members, curriculum collaborators

In my words

Some sessions required slowing down, rephrasing an idea, or finding a way around language and technical barriers. That was as important as covering the material.

The actual tutoring rooms

Across more than 60 Schoolhouse.world sessions, I've helped students work through algebra, pre-algebra, precalculus, SAT mathematics and statistics. Some sessions followed a clear lesson plan; others required me to respond to a misunderstanding or question I'd never anticipated. In either case, explaining something properly forced me to understand it better myself.

What inclusive dialogue looks like in practice

As Dialogue President, I encountered conversations where participants couldn't join easily because of language barriers, audio difficulties or unfamiliar technology. Sometimes I entered the room personally, spoke more slowly or used translation tools to make an exchange possible. I found that a facilitator's most important contribution can be creating the conditions for somebody else to speak.

A community rather than a statistic

I also contributed to blogging and peer-review work, reaching an advanced peer-review level. Providing feedback on how someone presents an argument requires a different kind of attention from answering a maths problem. Both activities taught me to be more careful about what I assume another person already knows.
